Introduction

For this project, I used the 2022–2023 Football Player Stats dataset. The dataset includes performance statistics for football players across European club teams.

The 3 questions:

  1. Do Brazilian players score more goals or italian players.

^^Do South American players score significantly more goals than European players? ^^(This will be done by grouping players into 2 (Europeans) and (South Americans)

  1. Is there a relationship between minutes played and goals scored?

  2. Do more completed passed into the 18 yard box result in more assists?

Data Description

Here are the key variables:

  • Name: Player’s name
  • Nation: Country of the player
  • Goals: Number of goals scored
  • MinutesPlayed: Total minutes played
  • Position: Playing position (e.g., FW, MF, DF)
  • Age: Player’s age
  • PPA : Completed passes into the 18-yard box
